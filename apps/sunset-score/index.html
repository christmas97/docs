FAQ

Is it free?

The app is free to download. Forecasts and alerts require a subscription. You’ll see the plans and price in the app before subscribing.

What does the score mean?

It estimates sunrise or sunset quality from 0 to 100. An 80 is a quality score, not an 80% chance of a beautiful sky. Weather can change, so check again before heading out.

Do I need an account?

No. Subscriptions are handled through the App Store or Google Play.
