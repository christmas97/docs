How it works

Record yourself

Keep your phone on a tripod or steady surface. Leave room in the frame for your double.

Record your double

Move to the other side of the frame. Keep the phone still and the lighting the same.

Paint and save

Paint where you want the second take to appear, then save the video to your photo library.

FAQ

Can the two versions of me overlap?

Leave a gap between them. If both recordings put you in the same spot, one version can cover the other at the painted boundary.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Recording, editing and export happen on your phone. Videos stay there unless you share them.

Is there a subscription?

No. Double You is a one-time purchase. Check your app store for the price in your region.
