FAQ

Sunset Score

Is it free?

The app is free to download. Forecasts and alerts require a subscription. You’ll see the plans and price in the app before subscribing.

What does the score mean?

It estimates how impressive a sunrise or sunset could be, from 0 to 100. A score over 70 points to a rare, remarkable sky worth going out for. It is a quality score, not a percentage chance. Weather can change, so check again before heading out.

What is behind the forecast?

We develop and test the model using thousands of sunrise and sunset observations from around the world. The focus is on telling ordinary skies from remarkable ones.

Do I need an account?

No. Subscriptions are handled through the App Store or Google Play.

Double You

Can the two versions of me overlap?

Leave a gap between them. If both recordings put you in the same spot, one version can cover the other at the painted boundary.

Does it upload my videos?

No. Recording, editing and export happen on your phone. Videos stay there unless you share them.

Is there a subscription?

No. Double You is a one-time purchase. Check your app store for the price in your region.
